Roster is a Shopify theme for salons, spas, barbers and studios, built for booking apps. Your services, team, hours and retail live on one store, and booking happens in the tool you already use.

Getting started

Three settings make the theme yours. Everything else is optional.

1. Set where Book goes

Open Theme settings → Booking and paste your booking link: a booking page from Fresha, Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius or Calendly, or a collection of your service products if you use a Shopify booking app. Every Book button in the theme (header, menu on phones, booking bar, team cards, service menu) follows this one setting.

2. Add your services as products

Create each service as a product. In the product's Shipping settings, turn off This is a physical product. Roster then shows it as a service everywhere: a "Book now" button, its length, and no quantity, stock or shipping details. See Services.

3. Add your hours and address

On the home page, the Hours and location section holds your weekly hours, holiday hours, phone and address. It shows a live "Open now" badge and tells search engines about your business.

Booking

Roster does not take bookings itself. It gives your booking tool the best possible home, in one of three ways.

A link to your booking tool

Set the link in Theme settings → Booking. Choose whether it opens in a new tab.

A Shopify booking app

Install the app, then add its block to the product page in the theme editor. Roster keeps the product form standard, never replaces the buy button when a variant changes, and refreshes its cart when the app adds a booking, so app labels such as "Select a time" stay in place.

An embedded booking widget

Create a page, choose the book template, and paste your tool's embed code into the Booking section. The section reserves space so the page does not jump while the widget loads. With no code or app block, it shows your Book button instead.

Services

A product that needs no shipping is a service. You can add a few optional details with product metafields (Settings → Custom data → Products → Add definition).

MetafieldTypeWhat it does
custom.duration_minutesIntegerShows the length ("1 hr 30 min") on cards, in the service menu and on the product page.
custom.price_fromTrue or falseShows "From" before the price, for services whose price depends on the client.
custom.book_labelSingle line textReplaces "Book now" on this service's button, for example "Reserve" or "Request a time".

To show a service like a retail product instead, turn off Theme settings → Booking → Show products that need no shipping as services.

Policy summary

Services show a short "Good to know" note under the buy button for your cancellation and deposit terms. Edit its text and link in the theme editor on the product page; it can also show on every product.

Cart

Slide-out cart or cart page

Theme settings → Cart → Cart type. The slide-out cart opens from the cart icon, after adding to cart, and when a booking app opens the cart. Without JavaScript the cart icon still leads to the cart page.

Quick add

Theme settings → Cart → Show quick add on product cards. Products with one variant add to the cart in one tap; products with options link to their page. Services keep their Book link, and quick add never appears on product pages, so booking apps always find the one product form there.

Team

The Team section shows portraits, roles, specialties and a "Book with" button for each person. Add people as blocks in the theme editor, or keep them once as metaobjects and show them anywhere.

Team members as metaobjects

  1. Settings → Custom data → Metaobjects → Add definition, type staff_member, with Storefronts access on.
  2. Add any of these fields (all optional): name, photo, role, specialties, bio, booking_link, portfolio_link.
  3. Add your team under Content → Metaobjects, then set the Team section's source to metaobjects.

FAQ

Do I need a booking app?

You need some booking tool, because Roster does not take bookings itself. That can be a Shopify booking app, or an outside tool such as Fresha, Vagaro, Square, GlossGenius or Calendly linked from Theme settings → Booking.

Which booking apps work with Roster?

Apps that add a block to the product page or open the cart through Shopify's standard storefront actions. Roster keeps the standard product form and buy button that these apps look for.

Can I sell retail products too?

Yes. Products that need shipping get the full retail product page, cart and quick add, side by side with your services.

How do memberships work?

The Packages section presents your plans; link each one to a product. For recurring billing, add a subscriptions app (Shopify Subscriptions is free) and give the product a selling plan. Roster shows the purchase options automatically.

Why does the Team section not show my metaobjects?

Check that the definition's type is staff_member, that Storefronts access is on, and that the section's source is set to metaobjects.

Can I change colors and fonts?

Yes, in Theme settings → Colors and Typography. Roster has five color schemes; every section lets you pick one.

How do I make checkout match?

Checkout is styled per store by Shopify: Settings → Checkout → Customize.

Can I translate the theme?

Yes. Every word in the theme can be changed in Online Store → Themes → Edit default theme content, or translated with Shopify's Translate and Adapt app.

Support

We answer every request within two business days. Please include your store URL. Fixes for theme bugs are always free.
