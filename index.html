Getting started

Roster works out of the box — these three steps activate the booking experience.

1 · Create your services as products

Each service (haircut, massage, class…) is a Shopify product. In the product's Inventory section untick Track quantity, and in Shipping untick This is a physical product. Use variants for tiers, e.g. 60 min / 90 min.

2 · Assign the service template

On each service product, set Theme template → service (right sidebar). This activates the booking form layout.

3 · Optional: duration & “from” pricing

Under Settings → Custom data → Products, add definitions custom.duration_minutes (integer) and custom.price_from (true/false). Roster shows durations in the service menu and prefixes prices with “From” automatically.

How bookings work

Customers pick a date, time window, staff member, and answer your intake questions on the product page. Their choices attach to the order as line-item properties — visible in the cart, at checkout, and on the order in your admin. No extra apps required.

Configure the form

Theme editor → a product using the service template → Booking form block: toggle date/time/staff, set your time windows. Add Intake question blocks for anything you need to know in advance (single line, multi-line, or checkbox; optionally required).

Staff & metaobjects

Enter each team member once; they appear in the booking dropdown and the Staff list section everywhere.

Set up the staff metaobject

  1. Settings → Custom data → Metaobjects → Add definition, named Staff member (type staff_member).
  2. Fields: name (single line text), photo (file), role, specialties, booking_link (URL).
  3. Enable Storefronts access in the definition's options.
  4. Add your team under Content → Metaobjects.

No metaobjects yet? The Staff list section also works with editor blocks, and the booking form accepts a comma-separated staff list.

FAQ

Do I need a booking app?

No. Roster records booking requests (date, time window, staff, intake answers) on the order itself. If you want live calendar availability, Roster works alongside appointment apps — their widgets drop into the product page via app blocks.

Where do I see a customer's booking details?

On the order in your Shopify admin — the details are listed under the line item.

Can I sell products as well as services?

Yes. Products without the service template get a standard product page — retail, gift cards, and services live side by side.

How do memberships work?

The Service packages section presents your plans; link each card to a product. For recurring billing, add a subscriptions app (e.g. Shopify Subscriptions, free) and attach a selling plan — Roster shows the purchase options automatically.

Why doesn't the staff dropdown show my team?

Check the metaobject definition uses field key name (or staff_member) and that Storefronts access is enabled. Roster also falls back to the Booking form block's comma-separated staff list.

Can I change colors and fonts?

Everything: Theme settings → Colors / Typography. Roster ships five coordinated schemes; all components follow your choices, including the booking form.

How do I style checkout to match?

Checkout is configured by Shopify per store: Settings → Checkout → Customize, set your brand colors and logo there.

Does Roster support other languages?

Roster inherits Shopify's translation system; all theme strings are translatable via the default translation editor.
